## Geography
---
### Wheylor
The region of [[Wheylor]] stretches from the [[Ashrock Peaks]] in the east to the [[Tremar Mountains]] in the west.
#### The Mittelmark
The [[Mittelmark]] makes up the south western third of Wheylor.
#### The Iothea valley
The [[Iothea Valley]] is a sprawling duchy to the east of the Mittelmark. Its capital city is [[Gord]].
### Varkareth
[[Varkareth]] is the eastern most region on Tir'Bras, sitting to the east of the Ashrock Peaks.
### Drakmora
[[Drakmora]] is the large island just south of the main continent that makes up Tir'Bras
